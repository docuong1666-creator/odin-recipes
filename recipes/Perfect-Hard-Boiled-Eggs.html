Perfect Hard-Boiled Eggs

Description

A recipe with lot of likes and reviews from allrecipes.com about how to make a Perfect Hard-Boiled Eggs

Time Estimated: 30-40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 tablespoon of salt
  • 1/8 cup distilled white vinegar
  • 3 cups water
  • 4 large eggs

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine salt, vinegar, and water in a large pot, and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, being careful not to crack them. Reduce the heat to a gentle boil, and cook for 10-14 minutes
  4. Once eggs have cooked, remove them from the hot water, and place into a container of ice water or cold, running water. Cool completely, about 15 minutes. Store in the refrigerator up to 1 week.
  5. Enjoy!
