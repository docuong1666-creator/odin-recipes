Egg in a Hole

Description

You want a quick and easy breakfast recipe, say no more, Egg in a Hole is definitely for you

Time Estimated: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1,5 teaspoons bacon grease
  • 1 slice bread
  • 1 egg
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Melt bacon grease in a nonstick pan over low heat.
  2. Cut a 2-inch(5cm) hole in the center of bread slice; place in the hot skillet and cook until lightly toasted, about 2 minutes
  3. Flip bread and crack egg into the hole; season with salt and pepper. Continue to cook until egg is cooked and mostly firm, flip again and cook 1 minute more
  4. Serve immediately
